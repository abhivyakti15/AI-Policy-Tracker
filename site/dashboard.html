What counts as "AI policy activity"

We include: state AI policies/missions/roadmaps; task forces, centres of excellence, AI cities or advisory councils; MoUs with companies or universities for AI; AI actually deployed in governance (health, agriculture, policing, education, citizen services); state budget lines for AI; and regulatory/ethics frameworks. We exclude AI mentioned only as a buzzword in unrelated announcements, private-sector-only AI news with no state government role, and national/central-government-only initiatives (e.g. IndiaAI Mission itself) unless a state is a clear active party.

Coverage & caveats

8 states, prioritised for depth over breadth (see README/RATIONALE in the repo). Dates are ISO where a source gave one; where sources only gave a month or year, we mark date_precision instead of guessing a day. Duplicate coverage of the same announcement across outlets was collapsed to one canonical record.
